## The Experiment 4 boundary note

The primary population comparison, H2b k2>k0, differs by exactly 5.0 points (4/40 = 10.0% against 9/60 = 15.0%). The frozen rule falsifies only differences strictly below 5 points. The first live analysis printed "falsified" because the floating-point difference was 0.0499999...; the code was fixed with a tolerance (commit 5939221, with a test) and the verdict is **inconclusive** (permutation p = 0.46). The family verdicts (H2a, H2b) are falsified under either reading, and no earlier verdict sits on the boundary.
